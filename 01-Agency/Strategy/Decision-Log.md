# Decision Log — Muchiri-OS

This document contains a record of important architectural and business decisions made during the operation of Muchiri-OS.

## Decision Format

Each entry follows this exact format:

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title

- **Decision:** What was decided
- **Context:** Why it was needed
- **Options:** What was considered
- **Chosen option:** What was selected
- **Reason:** Why
- **Consequences:** Expected outcomes
- **Related project:** Link if applicable
```

## Guidelines

- OpenCode should update this file when a major architectural or business decision is made.
- Before making a significant change, read existing entries to understand the pattern.
- Every decision should have a date, context, options, chosen option, reason, and consequences.
- Link related projects using wiki links: `[[06-Projects/Active/Project Name|Project Name]]`.
- Keep this file updated — do not leave decisions unwritten.
